Interactive lessons

Every exercise asks for your answer before it shows you anything, and a wrong answer gets feedback on the mistake it most likely came from. Plain pages, no accounts; progress stays in your browser. All four lessons.

Logic

Quantifier Gym

“For all” and “there exists”: why swapping their order changes the meaning. Play the ε–δ definition of a limit as a two-player game, then write the proof and let the page attack it.
Machine learning

Nearest Neighbours

Drag a point, change k, and watch the decision boundary redraw. Then step inside a cube and see why “nearest” stops meaning much in high dimensions.
Algorithms

Counting Steps

A run costs a number; a program costs a function. Count steps, plot the worst case, and play big-O as a game against a challenger. Built from my data-structures lecture.
Computability

Mechanical Moves

Turing machines in the notation my computability students use, stepped one move at a time. Write the shortest program for n + 1, then make one that never halts.
