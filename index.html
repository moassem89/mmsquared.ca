Mohammad Mahmoud

Assistant Professor, Teaching Stream, Computer ScienceUniversity of Toronto Mississauga

I teach the theory of computation: computability and complexity, algorithms, data structures, and numerical methods.

My mathematical home is computable structure theory, which asks how hard it is to tell when two infinite structures are the same. I did my PhD at the University of Waterloo with Barbara Csima. I also supervise undergraduate research teams. They have built a programming language for Turing machines, an AI-assisted theorem prover, and quantum-annealing experiments, and several of those projects have ended in peer-reviewed papers.
